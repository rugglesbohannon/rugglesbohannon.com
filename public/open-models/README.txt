OPEN MODELS — INTERACTIVE READING

Open index.html in a browser. The page works directly from disk and needs no installation or internet connection for the reading experience.

Alternatively, from this folder run:
python3 -m http.server 4173 --bind 127.0.0.1

Then visit http://127.0.0.1:4173

Scroll the narrative to highlight the corresponding tree section. Click a tree branch to jump to its passage. Switch reading modes at any time. The technical and plain versions follow the same seven stages.

The original diagram is included as original-diagram.png and linked under Sources & further reading. Sources link to their publishers and require internet access.

Everything is local. The page makes no external requests until you follow a source link. There are no external fonts, tracking, packages, or build steps.
